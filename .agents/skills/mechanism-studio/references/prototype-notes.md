Mechanism assets/adapters are not interchangeable. The restricted lock MCP was used for inspection/preview; engine/turbofan/quadruped Blender CLI builders must not be described as MCP-generated. Whether an MCP is connected is not a quality measure.

## Lessons to retain

- Check body/display units and full orientation conversion, including axes, cameras and explosion; mm/+Z to m/+Y changes the up axis.
- Check real shapes/joints. Correct formulas/IDs can still accompany a wrongly placed output paddle or leg center distance. Inspect meshes, bounds, poses and actual views.
- Separate working poses and observation offsets. Reassembly preserves phase/joint state. Automatic framing uses precomputed complete envelopes, not moving-frame auto-fit; visible bounds exclude line buffers/hidden solids.
- Shared viewer/voice/effects do not reimplement mechanism gates, crank equations or IK. Examples own those calculations and tests.
- Verify static redraw, offscreen/visibility return, resize and reduced motion. Changed numbers with a stale canvas is failure.
- Verify lesson identity and actual audio independently: stale text/audio, truncated streams, corrupt bytes and play rejection are separate cases. Credentials stay in generation, never the browser.
- STEP display count is not engineering count. Legacy KFL08's 22 source leaves/7 subassemblies and 35 fragments remain separately reported; absent mappings stay absent.
- Reuse import/evidence workflows, not specimen constants. A new source has its own provenance, digest and observed counts. Failed imports/publish batches retain previous successful assets.
- Inspect a real capture opening frame before a full recording. Pixel dimensions alone do not prove uncropped viewport/model presence. Sequential capture uses frame time; repeated seek may replace smooth transitions with cuts.

Recheck affected hashes, invariants and real frames after change. Shared tests do not prove every mechanism has certified dimensions or collision-free paths. Local .blend exports and generated speech can be present for private work without being included in the public Git package.
