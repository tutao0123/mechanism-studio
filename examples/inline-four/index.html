Teaching model: Bore 82 mm, stroke 90 mm and rod length 150 mm; illustrative displacement 1901 cm³. Ideal valve timing has no advance or overlap, and combustion pressure is not solved. The mechanical ignition event is at power-stroke TDC; its visible flash extends 14° afterward as a teaching window. Springs are static geometry, without elasticity.

Blue inflow and gold port-injection marks simplify premixed intake. The orange front, gas contrast and outgoing particles are process cues, not fuel quantity, droplet, pressure, temperature or CFD results.
