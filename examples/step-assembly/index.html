What this trial verifies

Existing-source import, unit conversion, OCCT grouping, derived identity, rest poses, picking and display explosion. Source relationships remain in the STEP and metadata. Original local transforms and instance mappings are not fully exposed by the importer.

Exact solid measurement, solid cutting, interference checks, constraint solving and parameter editing are not implemented. Explosion is an observation offset.
